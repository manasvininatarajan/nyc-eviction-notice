# nyc-eviction-notice

A plain-language crisis resource for NYC tenants who have received an eviction notice.

Built for caseworkers to share with clients facing housing emergencies. Includes:

- Step-by-step guidance written in plain language
- Real phone numbers for free legal aid by borough
- Emergency rental assistance programs
- Know-your-rights FAQ
- Interactive document checklist

**Live site:** https://yourusername.github.io/nyc-eviction-guide/

## Why I built this

A real resource for a real problem that affects thousands of New Yorkers every year — I wanted to take a stressful, jargon-heavy process and turn it into something a tenant or caseworker could actually use in the moment.

## Resources verified

July 2026 — NYC Legal Aid, HRA, Right to Counsel program

## Limitations

- **Not legal advice.** This is an informational guide only, not a substitute for consulting a licensed attorney or an official Right to Counsel provider about a specific case.
- **Contact info and program details can change.** Phone numbers, eligibility rules, and program funding for legal aid and rental assistance shift over time; anyone using this in a real housing emergency should confirm current details directly with the listed organizations before relying on them.
- **Borough-level coverage may be uneven.** Legal aid capacity and program availability differ by borough and can vary further within neighborhoods; a listed provider having availability isn't guaranteed.
- **Point-in-time snapshot.** Verified as of July 2026 — NYC housing policy and emergency assistance programs are subject to change, and this resource has no mechanism to auto-update when they do.

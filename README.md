# NYC Eviction Guide

A simple, plain-language resource for New Yorkers who have received an eviction notice.

Housing emergencies are stressful enough without having to decipher legal jargon. This guide walks tenants through what to do next, where to get help, and what documents to gather—all in language that's easy to understand.

It was designed with caseworkers, community organizations, and tenants in mind, so it can be shared quickly when someone needs help most.

**Live Demo:** https://manasvininatarajan.github.io/nyc-eviction-guide/

---

## What you'll find

- Step-by-step guidance for what to do after receiving an eviction notice
- Free legal aid resources organized by borough
- Emergency rental assistance programs
- A Know Your Rights FAQ
- An interactive checklist to help gather important documents

---

## Why I built this

Thousands of New Yorkers face eviction every year, and finding reliable information during a housing crisis can be overwhelming.

I wanted to build something that takes complicated legal processes and turns them into a resource that someone can actually use in the moment. Rather than sending people through dozens of government pages, this guide brings together practical next steps, trusted organizations, and essential information in one place.

The goal wasn't to replace legal advice—it was to make the first step a little less overwhelming.
